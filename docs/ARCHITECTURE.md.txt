## Human-in-the-loop

AI recommendations must not automatically execute critical business decisions.

A Supply Chain Manager reviews and approves the recommended recovery strategy.

## Prototype SAP Approach

The hackathon prototype may use synthetic enterprise data and simulated SAP actions.

The production architecture will support authorized SAP workflows/APIs through SAP BTP/integration services.
